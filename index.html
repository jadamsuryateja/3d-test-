Studio, not agency

Aerodyne is a small crew of designers and engineers who take on a handful of products a year — the way a squadron takes on a handful of pilots. Small enough to know every rivet, senior enough to skip the training wheels.

Founded on one rule: if it doesn't hold weight, it doesn't ship.
Climb — 1,200 ft

Discovery & strategy

Before we touch a single screen, we map the terrain: who's flying, what they're carrying, and what happens if a wing fails mid-air. Every product decision after this point traces back to that map.

The output isn't a deck. It's a flight plan your whole team can fly by.

Cruise — 4,000 ft

Interface & design

We design the way this airframe was built — visible seams, honest materials, nothing pretending to be something it isn't. Interfaces that make sense from the cockpit, not just the blueprint.

Typography, motion, and system — designed together, not in sequence.
